Why it matters

The collective risk model
Aggregate lossS = X1 + X2 + … + XN
Mean (needs N independent of X)E[S] = E[N] · E[X]
VarianceVar[S] = E[N]·Var[X] + Var[N]·E[X]2
Spearman’s test cannot reject independence, so frequency and severity can be modelled separately and recombined.

The frequency × severity model has a far heavier tail (TVaR99.5% ≈ €2.5bn) because its claim-level α = 1.38 gives infinite variance. The 180 observed months cannot show such events yet — a classic reason insurers buy excess-of-loss reinsurance.
